OPTIONAL POP-OUT CHARACTER IMAGES

For the real "character jumps out of the banner" effect, add a transparent PNG
(the character/person only, no background), tall like a portrait, ideally 800x1200.

Name it like the poster but ending in .png, for example:
  local-rings.png              (Rings)
  local-iron-man.png           (Iron Man)
  local-breaking-bad.png       (Breaking Bad)

Put it in this folder and refresh. It then appears in the hero banner, standing out above the frame
and moving with your mouse. Titles without a cutout show their poster floating out of the frame instead.
Background-removal tools such as remove.bg can make the transparent PNG.
Only the featured hero titles use it (Breaking Bad, Endgame, etc. rotate through).
